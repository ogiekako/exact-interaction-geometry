## 0. How to read this file

This file is a compressed historical reconstruction of the programme that became Exact Interaction Geometry (EIG). It is not a claim that the nine phase labels were canonical from the beginning, nor that the present EIG vocabulary was already available during the earlier work.

Three layers must be kept distinct.

1. **Original agenda.** What question, success criterion, or conjectural target was actually being pursued before the relevant result existed.
2. **Mathematical history.** What was actually proved, refuted, repaired, reduced, or left open.
3. **Later synthesis.** How the surviving mathematics was subsequently reorganized into EIG language.

The phase numbering is useful historically, but partly retrospective. In particular, some early work was not originally labeled “Phase I”, “Phase II”, and so on. The history is evidence for the present theory; it is not itself the definition of EIG.

The most important correction relative to the 2026-09-02 historical audit is recorded in §12: the original **Level-2** Phase-VIII-X target was subsequently closed on the sharp semantic-nervous class once the unnecessarily free `CLOSE` architecture was replaced by least closure under the **actual supplied semantic composition doctrine**. The stronger **Level-3** doctrine-free self-generation claim remains false.

# 1. The recurring problem

Across the phases, the same tension kept reappearing in different mathematical forms.

A system can be viewed through several layers:

```text
raw execution / composition
    -> admissible contexts
    -> observed response
    -> contextual quotient
    -> retained witness / factorization data
    -> interfaces / arities / shapes
    -> reconstruction / recognition
```

At each step, one can ask:

- which distinctions are forced by all admitted interactions;
- which distinctions can safely be quotiented away;
- which information is destroyed by an extensional quotient but must survive for later composition;
- whether the interface or arity system is intrinsic or merely supplied;
- whether reconstruction is unique, or only canonical up to equivalence, Cauchy completion, Morita equivalence, or moduli;
- whether the doctrine itself is recoverable, or must remain an external input.

The final EIG discipline was not obtained by choosing one preferred algebra in advance. It emerged because successive counterexamples made several initially plausible identifications untenable.

# 2. Phase I — all-context semantics

## Historical question

What does it mean for two interaction fragments to be operationally indistinguishable when the observer is allowed to place them into admitted feedback or continuation contexts?

## Mathematical lesson

The first stable principle was an **all-context response semantics**: if two fragments can never be distinguished by any admitted continuation, then the response semantics should identify them.

This is the ancestor of the later contextual quotient. It already shows that a raw state space or process presentation can contain distinctions with no operational content relative to a fixed doctrine.

## What failed

Several stronger intuitions did not survive:

- one scalar “repair width” or one-axis complexity parameter need not classify the system;
- finite semantic index does not imply every other structural aspect is effectively tame;
- raw recurrent or presentation complexity is not the same thing as observable response complexity;
- a terminal-looking calculation in one sector is not a global closure theorem.

## What survived

```text
admitted contexts + exact responses
    -> maximal observational indistinguishability relation.
```

This became the semantic base of later phases.

---

# 3. Phase II — future residuals and minimal sufficient state

## Historical question

Given a current fragment or history, what is the least information needed to predict all admissible future continuations?

## Mathematical lesson

The programme reached a Myhill–Nerode-like notion of **future equivalence**: two states are equivalent when every admitted future continuation gives the same response.

This produces a minimal future residual state and, in suitable sectors, a reduced observable action.

## Why this was not enough

Future equivalence can be perfect for prediction and still be too coarse for **composition**. Two fragments may have the same future response while carrying different internal factorization or witness structure needed when boundaries are reopened or pieces are glued.

This became one of the decisive EIG separations:

```text
minimal future state
    !=
lossless compositional interaction object.
```

---

# 4. Phase III — raw composition before projection

## Historical question

If semantic minimization forgets compositional information, where should composition live?

## Mathematical lesson

Composition must in general be performed at a **raw or witness-sensitive level before semantic projection**.

The key pattern is:

```text
raw fragments
    --compose-->
raw composite
    --project-->
semantic response
```

rather than attempting to reconstruct every composite law solely from already-quotiented semantic representatives.

## Consequence

This phase made explicit that extensional semantics and intensional realization are not interchangeable layers. A semantic quotient may be canonical while the compositional lift above it remains nontrivial.

---

# 5. Phase IV — carriers, charts, descent, and recanonicalization

## Historical question

How can locally meaningful interaction descriptions be assembled when different presentations or carriers represent the same extensional object?

## Mathematical lesson

The relevant structure became **local-to-global**:

- local carriers or charts;
- transition data;
- compatibility conditions;
- descent/gluing;
- recanonicalization after changing presentation.

The correct output is often not one chosen representative. It may be a groupoid, torsor, chart atlas, or moduli object.

## Lasting distinction

```text
canonical quotient
    does not imply
canonical representative.
```

This is one origin of the later insistence that residual moduli be treated as mathematical output rather than as a defect to hide.

---

# 6. Phase V — interface before scalar valuation

## Historical question

Can one identify the structural interface through which interactions pass before collapsing everything to a scalar objective or response?

## Mathematical lesson

The programme learned to separate an **interface object** from the value ultimately read from it.

A scalar can be sufficient for one query while erasing multiplicity, provenance, path, phase, or other structure needed for a different continuation. Therefore the meaningful object may be an exact interface through which many response maps factor.

## Lasting distinction

```text
response value
    !=
interface carrying all reusable interaction information.
```

This later became central to EIG’s exact-interface and residual-fibre language.

# 7. Phase VI — semantic quotient versus lossless interaction reduction

## Historical question

Can the observational quotient itself serve as the complete interaction object?

## Answer

In general, no.

The historical programme had initially aimed at stronger universal reductions. Later comparison with standard categorical machinery and explicit counterexamples forced a contraction of that ambition.

The surviving principle is that an exact semantic quotient can be **minimal as an observational state space** while still failing to preserve all information required for:

- witness-sensitive composition;
- factorization multiplicity;
- provenance;
- local reopening;
- cross-process morphisms.

## Final lesson

One must distinguish:

```text
semantic minimization
    from
lossless interaction reduction.
```

The latter may require fibres or displayed structure over the former.

# 8. Phase VII — arities, probes, and the density/selection split

## Historical question

If a world can be reconstructed from a family of probes or arities, does the world itself canonically determine that family?

## Mathematical lesson

A fixed dense family may be sufficient for reconstruction, but **density does not select the probes**.

Multiple different dense families can reconstruct the same world. Therefore a theorem of the form

```text
chosen probes are dense
    ->
world is reconstructed
```

is weaker than a theorem saying the probes themselves are intrinsically recognized.

## Lasting distinction

```text
density / sufficiency
    !=
intrinsic arity selection.
```

This directly motivated Phase VIII’s question: *who chose the arities?*

# 9. Phase VIII — from atoms to Tiny/Cauchy roots and semantic shape generation

## Original ambition

The strongest early Phase-VIII picture was roughly:

```text
world
    -> primitive interactions / atoms
    -> free closure
    -> canonical shape theory
    -> interaction nerve
    -> reconstructed objects and morphisms.
```

This was too strong in several ways.

## Failures that mattered

The historical audits exposed distinct problems:

- arbitrary or marked atoms can encode the desired answer;
- a marked reduction can be circular;
- ambient Yoneda full faithfulness does not recover restricted process Homs;
- disconnected primitive probes do not force mixed bridge structure;
- tabulation or presentation choices need not be uniquely contractible;
- specieswise response images need not preserve operations;
- freely generated mixed syntax can manufacture semantic equations and coherence obligations that the supplied world never required.

The important methodological correction was: **do not guess a primitive basis first**.

## Intrinsic replacement

In presheaf-type sectors the intrinsic small root is given by **Tiny/Cauchy objects**, not by an arbitrary chosen atom basis. This leads to a basis-free generic root, followed by exact reduction/completion.

But Tiny alone still does not reconstruct the world. One needs a generated semantic shape theory and then a separate recognition theorem.

## 2026-09-03 correction

The decisive later correction was to abandon the unnecessarily strong free mixed `CLOSE` construction.

Instead, with an exact interaction/composition doctrine supplied independently, take the **least closure inside that semantic doctrine under the actual typed constructors that the doctrine really has**.

```text
whole-doctrine REDUCE
    -> intrinsic Tiny/Cauchy root
    -> exact completion
    -> least actual-constructor semantic closure
    -> full semantic theory on generated shapes.
```

This closes shape generation on the stated small coherent semantic-constructor class without inventing a free syntax that later needs to be proved fully faithful.

## Remaining recognition gate

Generated shapes need not be dense enough to reconstruct the whole semantic world. The exact obstruction is the noninvertibility of the **density counit**, equivalently failure of the generated-shape nerve to be fully faithful.

Thus:

```text
intrinsic shape generation: doctrine-relative positive theorem;
full reconstruction: exactly on the nervous / dense subclass.
```

# 10. Phase IX — doctrine-relative fixed point and the no-go ceiling

## Original ambition

The strongest Phase-IX dream was an absolute extractor/realizer fixed point such as

```text
World -> unique Interaction(World).
```

## No-go

This is false in general.

The same ordinary extensional map world can support inequivalent interaction doctrines. The simplest conceptual witness is the distinction between a witness-preserving span-like doctrine and a witness-forgetting relation-like doctrine: the extensional maps may agree while the intensional interaction data do not.

Likewise, native ternary structure and binary factorized presentations can generate the same extensional behavior while retaining different primitive interaction information.

Therefore bare extensional semantics does not canonically select:

- an execution grammar;
- a witness doctrine;
- a primitive arity system;
- locality;
- higher coherence;
- a unique ontology.

## Positive endpoint

Once the doctrine is fixed, however, a strong doctrine-relative canonicality stack survives:

```text
contextual separated reflection
    -> Tiny/Cauchy root
    -> universal exact reduction
    -> least actual-constructor semantic closure
    -> full semantic restriction
    -> comparison / failure objects
    -> recognition gates
    -> equivalence / Cauchy / Morita / moduli output.
```

The resulting fixed point is **doctrine-relative and equivalence-relative**, not absolute.

# 12. Exact status correction: Level 2 versus Level 3

This is the most important update after the 2026-09-02 historical audit.

## Level 2

Input includes an independently specified exact interaction/composition doctrine.

The question is then:

```text
fixed doctrine
    -> intrinsic reduced root
    -> minimal composition-forced shape theory
    -> associated semantic theory
    -> nerve / recognition theorem.
```

The 2026-09-03 semantic-closure re-adjudication closes this line on the sharp class where the intrinsic recognition gates hold.

The canonical generated shape theory exists on the stated small coherent semantic-constructor class. Full reconstruction holds on the proper subclass satisfying semantic density/nervousness; outside it the failure object is the noninvertible density counit.

## Level 3

Input is only a bare extensional world, and the doctrine itself is supposed to be recovered canonically.

```text
bare extensional world
    -/-> unique interaction doctrine.
```

This remains false in general. The Span/Rel and presentation/witness counterexamples are not repaired by the Level-2 theorem.

## Historical consequence

Older statements in the 2026-09-02 audit saying that `VIII-U`, `VIII-X`, or the Phase-VIII-X fixed point was “OPEN” should be read as correct **at that audit cutoff**. They are superseded for the original Level-2 target by the 2026-09-03 correction, but not for the stronger doctrine-free Level-3 target.

# 13. What is actually canonical

The historical record supports a hierarchy rather than one undifferentiated claim of uniqueness.

## Canonical relative to a fixed doctrine

Depending on the sector and hypotheses, the following can be canonical or characterized by universal properties:

- all-context observational quotient;
- minimal future residual state;
- contextual separated reflection;
- Tiny/Cauchy root in the relevant presheaf/generic setting;
- universal exact reduction/completion;
- least closure under the doctrine’s actual constructors;
- the full semantic subtheory on independently generated shapes;
- canonical comparison maps and exact failure fibres;
- the appropriate equivalence/Morita/moduli class of reconstructions.

## Not canonically determined in general

The following are not forced by bare extensional data without extra hypotheses or supplied doctrine:

- a unique witness-rich interaction doctrine;
- a unique primitive presentation;
- a unique dense probe family merely from density;
- a unique strict representative rather than a moduli object;
- a universal scalar invariant controlling every application;
- a universal doctrine-free objecthood selector;
- a unique locality or ontology.

This boundary is not a weakness to be hidden. It is one of the main mathematical outputs of the programme.

# 14. What EIG became

The mature interpretation is not merely “take contextual quotients”, and not merely “objects emerge from interaction”.

A more faithful summary is:

```text
1. specify the interaction doctrine;
2. quotient exactly what all admitted contexts erase;
3. retain witness/factorization information needed by reopened interaction;
4. compose at the correct raw or semantic level;
5. generate only structure forced by the actual doctrine;
6. expose comparison defects rather than hiding them;
7. separate construction from recognition;
8. state the exact residual fibre or moduli when recognition fails;
9. claim canonicality only at the equivalence level actually proved.
```

This is why EIG is better viewed as a **discipline of exact interaction-relative reconstruction and obstruction** than as one universal algebra.

# 15. Meta-EIG and the discovery process

The Phase-VIII-X episode also produced a methodological lesson.

The successful discovery pattern was repeatedly:

```text
candidate representation
    -> expose to independently admitted contexts
    -> compute exact comparison defect
    -> retain only distinctions the defect proves necessary
    -> repeat until stable.
```

This mirrors EIG itself: a distinction deserves to survive precisely when an admitted interaction can detect or require it.

The important methodological warning is therefore:

> Do not guess the hidden mathematical structure first.

Instead, freeze the doctrine, start with the weakest candidate representation, force it through adversarial contexts, extract the exact failure, refine minimally, and only then ask whether the stable object admits a small atomic, familial, polynomial, analytic, Cauchy, or Morita presentation.

This process changed the Phase-VIII target materially:

```text
initial intuition:
    canonical atoms -> free closure -> unique shape theory

final architecture:
    intrinsic Tiny/Cauchy root
      -> exact reduction
      -> semantic generated closure
      -> recognition gates
      -> Cauchy/Morita/moduli canonicality.
```

The latter is weaker than the initial slogan in literal uniqueness, but mathematically stronger because every surviving layer has an exact role and every known failure has an explicit boundary.

# 16. Relation to later EIG work

Two major downstream programmes should not be projected backward into the Phase I–IX history.

## Beyond-Core / North-Star programme

Later work asks whether interactions can generate not only residual quotients but also nontrivial shape systems, laws, and mathematical theories — summarized by slogans such as

```text
interactions generate shapes;
shapes generate mathematics.
```

This is a **future research programme built on the Core**, not evidence that Phase I–IX already proved such a universal theorem.

## Physics / ORPP programme

The physics-facing programme applies the same discipline to symmetry, charge, locality, objecthood, deformation response, world reconstruction, and residual physical moduli.

Its core firewall is:

```text
semantic reconstruction
    != physical realizability
    != exhaustion
    != prediction
    != world selection.
```

Those results and open problems belong to downstream EIG applications, not to the historical proof that the Phase I–IX sequence itself was inevitable.
