## Finite future-legality theorem

For a finite many-sorted alphabet of deterministic partial Presburger letters, the following are equivalent:

1. exact labelled legal-future equivalence has finite index;
2. there is a finite Presburger deterministic stable partition;
3. the least many-sorted Boolean algebra closed under guarded partial-map inverse images is finite.

The guarded preimage is

```text
Pre_a(S) = G_a intersect F_a^{-1}(S),
```

and the truth-atom refinement halts exactly when the future index is finite.

## OCN affine lifting theorem

Under counter/control-only one-counter-net legality, total integral-affine payload, strictly positive public clock, complete endpoints, and complete observable/scalable exposure, exact response is Presburger iff

```text
minimal reachable/future-observable coefficient action is finite,
initialized signed Z-rational intercept is O(|w|+1).
```

The criterion is on the observable minimal action, not the raw affine monoid.
