# Theorem dossiers

A dossier is the audit-facing unit of the Book. Each dossier answers six questions:

1. What exact domain is being quantified over?
2. What is the theorem statement?
3. What are the load-bearing proof steps?
4. What tempting stronger theorem is false?
5. What does the verifier check?
6. Where is the immutable proof/audit provenance?
