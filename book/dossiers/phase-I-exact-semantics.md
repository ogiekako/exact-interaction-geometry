# Dossier I — exact feedback semantics and the effective no-go boundary

**Status:** `AUDITED / ACCEPTED`.

## Domain

Finite-interface recurrent presentations in the declared atlas/query setting, with exact finite-path closure under the retained feedback operations and a declared finitary existential-positive observer family `O`.

## Theorem spine

Let `FbCl(T)` denote the exact many-sorted finite-path feedback closure. Contextual indistinguishability under the declared observer family is a congruence `==_O`, and

```text
Omega_O(T) = FbCl(T) / ==_O
```

is the least-information fully abstract observer object: raw behaviours have the same image exactly when no allowed context/observer distinguishes them.

Separately, one transparent scalar family has both the Presburger and non-Presburger truth sides non-c.e. Hence no exhaustive sound c.e. LOW/HIGH proof atlas can classify the unrestricted family.

## Proof skeleton

1. Build the exact finite-path feedback algebra before quotienting.
2. Define observer equivalence by star-free one-hole tests.
3. Use finite unfolding/closure to show this is already the largest observer-invisible congruence for the feedback signature.
4. Quotient to obtain full abstraction.
5. Embed the fixed universal scalar family and prove the two truth sides are non-c.e.; exhaustive c.e. routing would enumerate a forbidden side.

## Boundary

- `Omega_O` is not a substitute for a lossless structural representation when contextual equivalence is nontrivial.
- The non-c.e. theorem forbids an exhaustive effective classifier, not exact mathematical semantics.
- Positive structural subdomains remain possible and motivate Phase II.

## Verification role
