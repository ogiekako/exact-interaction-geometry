# Dossier VI — realizability by execution categories

**Status:** generic shell `KNOWN / PRIOR ART`; reduced process-specific theorem is the substantive frontier.

## Generic construction

Given

```text
p : E -> B,
```

let `E_b` be the fibre over `b`, and for each boundary arrow `f:b->c` define

```text
M_f(x,y) = { u:x->y in E | p(u)=f }.
```

Composition of lifts induces normal-lax `Prof` structure. Conversely, the generalized Bénabou--Grothendieck construction reconstructs a category over `B` from such a normal-lax profunctor-valued functor, up to the standard variance convention.

The pseudo case corresponds to the Conduche/exponentiable exact-middle-factorization sector. The correct uniqueness is up to the coend/fibre-zigzag equivalence, not strict unique lifting.

## Why this is not yet the project theorem

A raw profunctor may retain the entire global state as a witness. The Grand Book wants a

```text
reduced + noncopying + cofinal + feedback-stable + reconstructive
```

interaction stack generated by the actual structural interfaces. Generic Grothendieck reconstruction proves realizability once such data are given; it does not prove the compression.

## Novelty boundary

Do not claim the `Cat/B <-> lax Prof` or Conduche shell as new. The programme-specific bridge is the derivation of the correct reduced interaction data from partial legality, recurrent feedback, observers/actions, and noncopying structural ports.
