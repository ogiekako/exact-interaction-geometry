### I.1 Exact finite-path observer quotient — **AUDITED / ACCEPTED**
For a declared finite-interface recurrent atlas and a finitary existential-positive observer family `O`, exact finite-path feedback semantics admits a contextual congruence whose quotient

```text
Omega_O = FbCl(T) / ==_O
```

is fully abstract for the declared observers: two raw behaviours have the same image exactly when no allowed one-hole context/observer distinguishes them. The quotient is taken after raw composition/feedback.

### I.2 Two-sided non-r.e. optimality theorem — **AUDITED / ACCEPTED**
Inside one fixed transparent scalar family, the instances whose exact response is Presburger and the instances whose response is non-Presburger are both not computably enumerable. Consequently no exhaustive sound c.e. LOW/HIGH proof atlas can classify the unrestricted family.

**Structural consequence.** Opacity is a theorem-imposed endpoint, not merely failure of the present compiler.

## Phase IV — exact separators and the Grand Book



with canonical bijection

```text
C(M_Gamma) ~= disjoint-union_(alpha,beta) P_S(alpha,beta).
```

Relative to the fixed private projections, `P_S` is the reduced universal quotient of every exact extensional separator code.

The exact dynamic interface is therefore dependent/proarrow-like; a sufficient deterministic completion is larger than the state-only base.

## Phase V — interaction geometry

### V.1 Exact interaction precedes valuation — **ACCEPTED SYNTHESIS**
The invariant datum is the exact compatibility kernel/proarrow and its factorization/compression structure. Rank, dimension, log-cardinality, entropy, communication cost, and Presburger dimension are valuations of that structure, not interchangeable definitions of it.

### V.2 Hidden-middle support-flatness no-go — **AUDITED / ACCEPTED**
Every binary relation factors through its witness set as a composite of a function graph and a converse-function graph. Both legs are difunctional. Therefore no proper class of relations containing functions and their converses and closed under ordinary relational composition can use bare support-difunctionality as its universal notion of flatness.

### V.3 Equivalence-relation interaction CRT — **AUDITED / ACCEPTED**
For equivalence relations `alpha,beta` on `X`, the canonical map

```text
X/(alpha intersect beta)
 -> X/alpha x_(X/(alpha join beta)) X/beta
```

is bijective iff `alpha beta = beta alpha`.

### V.4 Modular uncrossing theorem — **AUDITED / ACCEPTED**
For observation kernel congruences with

```text
theta_(A union B)=theta_A meet theta_B,
theta_A join theta_B <= theta_(A intersect B),
```

and a monotone modular valuation `d`, the rank-like function `r(A)=D-d(theta_A)` is submodular with exact slack

```text
d(theta_(A intersect B)) - d(theta_A join theta_B) >= 0.
```

The associated symmetric connectivity function has two positive shore residuals. Permutable/Mal'tsev settings provide a clean exact common-interface sector.

### V.5 Universal submodularity is false — **REFUTATION**
Already the Presburger relation

```text
x1=x2 OR x3=x4
```

violates the natural exact cut-channel submodularity inequality. Later affine fixtures show failure inside still more structured sectors. Submodularity is a structural phase, not an axiom of exact interaction.
