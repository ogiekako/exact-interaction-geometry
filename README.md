# Exact Interaction Geometry

**Canonical semantic closure and reconstruction under declared interaction doctrines.**

Exact Interaction Geometry (EIG) studies what mathematical structure is determined once an interaction doctrine has been stated precisely: which contexts and observations are admitted, which distinctions are operationally invisible, which semantic constructions are genuine, and which equivalences count as the same theory.

The canonical foundation is [`EIG_CORE.md`](EIG_CORE.md). Its central fixed-doctrine statement is

$$
\mathrm{EIGCore}(D)=\mathrm{Cl}_D(R_D).
$$

Here `D` is the declared interaction doctrine, `R_D` is an intrinsic root extracted **after contextual reduction**, and `Cl_D` is the least simultaneous semantic closure generated by the doctrine. Under the stated Moore-family / monotone-generation hypotheses, this Core is the least admissible `D`-closed semantic subtheory containing the root.

The point is not that every interaction problem has one doctrine-free canonical answer. The theory separates three questions:

1. **Fixed doctrine:** what semantic structure is forced by `D`?
2. **Reconstruction:** which richer structures can be recognized or recovered from that Core under additional exactness hypotheses?
3. **Doctrine dependence:** what, if anything, descends after the doctrine itself is forgotten?

## Canonical construction

The generic architecture is

```text
RAW INTERACTION DATA
        |
        v
ADMITTED CONTEXTS / OBSERVATIONS
        |
        v
CONTEXTUAL REDUCTION
        |
        v
REDUCED SEMANTIC WORLD W_D
        |
        v
INTRINSIC ROOT R_D
        |
        v
LEAST SIMULTANEOUS D-CLOSURE
        |
        v
EIGCore(D) = Cl_D(R_D)
        |
        v
ASSOCIATED STRUCTURAL THEORY Theta_D
```

The order matters. In general one may not extract a raw root first and assume that reduction preserves it. Root preservation is a separate exactness condition.

For an admissible seed `S`, the closure is characterized abstractly by

$$
\mathrm{Cl}_D(S)
=
\bigwedge\{B : S\le B,\; B\text{ is }D\text{-closed}\}.
$$

Thus an element belongs to the closure exactly when it belongs to every admissible `D`-closed upper model of the seed. Nonmembership therefore has an exact stable countermodel. No preferred order of semantic generators is required for existence of the Core; an ordered or staged construction is an additional factorization theorem and needs its own preservation and coherence hypotheses.

## Across doctrines

The fixed-doctrine theorem does not silently choose a doctrine.

Under the explicit transport hypotheses, doctrine morphisms induce lax comparison maps between closures, and doctrine equivalences transport the closure modality and Core at the declared equivalence level.

If doctrine information is then forgotten along a map schematically written

$$
U:\mathcal D\to\mathcal S,
$$

two different problems appear:

```text
choose a doctrine coherently
    = a section / selection problem for U

find one Core depending only on the forgotten semantics
    = a descent / factorization problem for the Core functor K
```

These problems are independent. A doctrine may fail to admit a canonical selection while its Core still descends, or a canonical doctrine selection may exist while the Core does not descend through the forgotten semantics.

When no point-valued doctrine-free answer is justified, the lossless output is the full doctrine fibre together with the EIG data actually proved on each doctrine, rather than an arbitrarily selected representative.

## Reconstruction is downstream

The Core theorem is deliberately separated from stronger reconstruction claims. The following are **not** consequences of least semantic closure alone:

- density or a nerve theorem;
- recognition of an essential image;
- exact recovery of operations from restrictions;
- witness multiplicity, provenance, phase, or higher coherence from scalar responses;
- Beck--Chevalley or interchange invertibility;
- process/model realization;
- a doctrine-free objecthood or locality selector.

Such results are additional theorems or certificates. This separation replaces the earlier attempt to package contextual reduction, objecthood, witness reconstruction, descent, and full realization into one universal reconstruction target. What is generic is the Core and its exact canonicality/no-go architecture; richer reconstruction is doctrine- and application-dependent.

## Canonicality and no-go statements

EIG treats canonicality as a property of a **specified solution problem**, not of an undifferentiated moduli space.

For a fully specified selection, descent, reconstruction, presentation, or coherence problem:

- an empty solution space means the requested solution does not exist;
- a contractible solution space gives canonicality up to coherent choice;
- a nonempty noncontractible solution space means the stated conditions do not determine a unique solution, but does not rule out a stronger independently justified universal property;
- when invariance is required, the relevant obstruction is the invariant solution space (locally a homotopy-fixed-point problem, and across noninvertible maps a global-section problem when the solution assignment is functorial).

This is the sense in which the positive and negative boundaries match: fixed-doctrine Core is an initial least-closure problem with a canonical solution, while doctrine selection and doctrine-free descent are separate solution problems that may or may not have invariant solutions.

## Exact calibrations

### Finite response tables

For a finite response table

```text
M : X x Y -> K,
```

equality of response rows gives the coarsest surjective deterministic exact interface. If `q : X -> I` is any other surjective exact interface and `pi : X -> R(M)` is the canonical row quotient, then

```text
pi = h o q
```

for a unique `h : I -> R(M)`.

This is the smallest exact calibration of contextual reduction. If interfaces retain latent witnesses instead of only deterministic residual states, the relevant complexity becomes doctrine-dependent factorization data rather than one universal scalar dimension.

See [`theory/01-finite-exact-interactions.md`](theory/01-finite-exact-interactions.md).

### Categories from untyped execution

Let `C` be a small category. Erase the object set and all source/target and Hom labels, retain the raw arrows, and totalize execution by sending noncomposable pairs to an absorbing failure symbol. If success/failure is admitted as an observation, two-sided continuation profiles recover endpoint types. Retaining the raw arrows as witness fibres then recovers Hom sets, identities, and composition.

```text
untyped totalized execution
+ one-bit continuation success
+ retained raw-arrow witnesses
    -> objects + typing + Hom sets + identities + composition
```

This is an exact recognition theorem using classical category-consolidation / semigroup ingredients. The execution law is supplied; it is not reconstructed from bare scalar observations.

See [`theory/02-category-reconstruction.md`](theory/02-category-reconstruction.md).

## Conventional mathematics generated by the programme

### Two-state max-plus comparison

For max-plus automata over `Z_max`, Daviaud--Guillon--Merlet (MFCS 2017) proved bounded-state comparison undecidable at 553 states and left the range `2..552` open. The manuscript in this repository proves the `d=2` case in the stronger asymmetric form:

> If `B` has at most two states, then for arbitrary finite max-plus `A`, pointwise comparison `[[A]] <= [[B]]` is decidable.

The proof isolates the unique unbounded two-state projective gap. Beyond an explicit threshold, every nondead transition is exactly of type `PROPAGATE`, `FORGET`, or `READ-AND-FORGET`. A transition whose output depends on the unbounded magnitude cannot also propagate that magnitude to the future. This yields a functional one-counter realization, followed by Parikh semilinearity and Presburger arithmetic.

### Binary-Kronecker calibration

The programme also found an explicit `5 x 5` binary matrix with

```text
rank_bin(A) = 5,
rank_bin(A tensor A) <= 24 < 25.
```

This is retained only as a search/calibration example. Yaroslav Shitov had already publicly posted a different `5 x 5` `5 -> 24` example on 2026-07-25, so no novelty claim is made for binary-rank Kronecker nonmultiplicativity.

## What EIG does not claim

Many constituent tools are standard mathematics: contextual equivalence and syntactic minimization, Cauchy/Karoubi completion, dense generators and nerve theorems, distributive laws, descent, factorization ranks, equipments/proarrows, Kan extensions, and homotopy-fixed-point or section machinery. See [`PRIOR_ART.md`](PRIOR_ART.md) and [`CONTEMPORARY_NEIGHBORS.md`](CONTEMPORARY_NEIGHBORS.md).

In particular, EIG does **not** claim generically that:

- bare probabilities or distinguishability determine an execution doctrine;
- contextual reduction preserves an independently chosen raw root;
- least closure implies density or full reconstruction;
- all semantic constructors admit one canonical monadic packaging;
- universal constructions automatically make Beck--Chevalley/interchange maps invertible;
- a noncontractible raw moduli space alone rules out every canonical selector;
- forgetting the doctrine leaves one canonical point-valued Core;
- an interaction structure automatically determines physical locality, microscopic atomization, or metric geometry.

The detailed theorem, counterexample, and scope boundaries are in [`EIG_CORE.md`](EIG_CORE.md).
