## Explicitly open / conjectural

The following are **not** promoted as theorems here:

1. **WEIR in broad natural classes.** No general theorem yet derives response algebra, object/interface locus, maps, witnesses, Hom reconstruction, doctrine change, and descent in one package.
2. **Doctrine-internal `Sharp_D`.** A universal method selecting the right stable interfaces from interaction data alone is not known.
3. **Witness-enriched descent beyond the category calibration.** Multiplicity, provenance, cocycles, and higher coherence require more than scalar contextual quotients.
4. **Native port/arity/wiring recognition.** It is not established that all open-interface structure can be recovered without a supplied interface ledger.
5. **Blind cross-domain extraction and unicity.** A single frozen extractor has not yet been shown to recover the expected object/map theories in several distant domains without being told the answers.
6. **Literal uniqueness.** Cauchy, Morita, presentation, and doctrine moduli may be unavoidable; the correct theorem may be moduli-valued rather than unique on the nose.

## Not imported from the research ledger

The underlying research ledger contains many later or broader EIG branches: recurrent-process structure, tensor/doctrine refinements, physical calibrations, Graph-Minors-style obstructions, pasting/preclone recognition, Tucker/nonnegative-rank applications, AQFT/OAQEC directions, and other theorem candidates.

They are **deliberately absent** from this public core unless they are needed to state or test the foundational EIG claim. In particular, an audit-pending result does not become public-core mathematics merely because it is newer. The source-pair dossier is the exception only because it is a small, self-contained external falsification target with explicit certificates and a strong provenance firewall.

## Verification boundary

`make verify` runs finite regression checks for the category reconstruction theorem. `make verify-discoveries` runs exact checks for the external discovery candidates. Passing a program is evidence about the encoded statement; it is not by itself a proof of an infinite theorem and says nothing about historical novelty.

## Historical novelty boundary

Many EIG ingredients are established mathematics. The repository currently claims priority only for this dated formulation and research programme, not historical priority over any classical ingredient. The source-pair discovery dossier is likewise withheld from an unqualified priority claim until the dedicated post-2018 literature audit is complete.
