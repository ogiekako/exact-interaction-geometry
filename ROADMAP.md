# Roadmap

Only the main foundational gates belong here. This is not a catalogue of every application generated by the research programme.

## R0 — identify the maximal invariant and residual fibre

Before trying to reconstruct a preferred target, formulate the exact identifiability problem for the declared operational doctrine.

A successful theorem should state:

```text
what the admitted responses determine;
what target realizations remain indistinguishable;
which equivalence/moduli describe that fibre;
which additional operational resource, if any, reduces it.
```

A sharp no-go or residual-moduli theorem counts as success. Do not silently strengthen the doctrine merely to recover the originally desired target.

## R1 — derive the interface locus

Find natural doctrine-relative hypotheses under which interaction itself selects the appropriate stable/sharp interface objects.

Success must do more than rename supplied objects as identities. It should recover a predicate such as `Sharp_D` from execution, tests, adjoints/order/causality, or other doctrine-internal structure.

Calibrations should include at least categories, relations, and a genuinely non-relational doctrine.

## R2 — witness-enriched exact reconstruction

Scalar contextual quotients lose multiplicity, provenance, cocycles, phases, and higher comparison data. Build the minimal enriched reduction that retains exactly the witness information distinguishable by reopened boundaries.

Target properties:

```text
exact contextual reduction
+ witness fibres
+ composition
+ local-to-global descent
+ explicit Hom reconstruction.
```

A theorem that assumes the desired Hom sets or apex objects as input does not close this gate.

## R3 — recognize ports, arities, and pasting

Determine how much open-interface structure is intrinsic.

There is already a basic obstruction: an algebra's full term clone need not remember which operations were historically declared primitive. Therefore the goal is not arbitrary generator recovery. The meaningful question is whether an operationally canonical interface/arity/pasting calculus can be reconstructed, and what irreducible interface ledger must remain primitive.

Audit-pending results from the research ledger are intentionally not promoted here.

## R4 — blind cross-domain calibration

Freeze the **meta-rule/schema** before applying it to known domains. It may be parameterized by the declared doctrine, but it must not be retuned using the expected target answer.

Minimum stress tests should span mathematically distant sectors, for example:

```text
small categories;
relations / allegorical maps;
linear or convex systems;
recurrent/open processes;
one witness-rich physical or operator-algebraic doctrine.
```

A mismatch is evidence, not something to patch by silently changing the extractor after seeing the target.

## R5 — unicity or moduli

Literal uniqueness is probably too strong. Determine the sharp invariant notion under which two admissible reconstructions agree:

```text
isomorphism?
equivalence?
Cauchy completion?
Morita equivalence?
doctrine-relative moduli/groupoid?
```

The theorem should identify the unavoidable ambiguity rather than hide it. Identifiability and canonical selection must be kept distinct.

## R6 — external mathematical and novelty audit

Before any field-level claim is made:

- obtain specialist review from category/semigroup/realisability experts;
- perform a dedicated literature audit around interaction-first foundations;
- independently reconstruct the central proofs from the public statements;
- keep refutations and scope repairs visible;
- distinguish novelty of a theorem from novelty of a programme-level framing.

The publication standard is a narrower theorem with a clean boundary, not a stronger slogan.
