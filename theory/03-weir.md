# WEIR — Witness-Enriched Interaction Reconstruction

**Status: CONJECTURAL FOUNDATIONAL PROGRAMME.**

WEIR is the current sharp form of the general EIG question. It is intentionally stated as acceptance criteria rather than as a theorem already obtained.

## 1. Candidate laboratory

A doctrine `D` should determine a natural class of exact interaction laboratories `L_D` whose primitive data may include:

1. untyped or locally typed interaction fragments and admissible execution/pasting;
2. closed continuation experiments and exact response values;
3. witness-valued response/provenance when an internal boundary can be reopened;
4. recodings under which admitted experiments are required to be invariant.

The expected objects, maps, generators, Hom sets, path categories, span apexes, sectors, or other target structures must **not** simply be supplied as the answer.

The hoped-for reconstruction has the schematic form

```text
L_D
  -> Syn_D(L)
  -> Intf_D(L)
  -> Map_D(L)
```

with a witness/descent enhancement.

## 2. Acceptance gates

### W1 — exact contextual algebra

There is a coarsest composition-stable reduction preserving every admitted continuation response.

### W2 — derived objecthood

Interfaces/objects arise from interaction-internal stable/sharp/retract data selected by the doctrine, not from a supplied object list.

### W3 — derived maps

Map-like interactions are characterized internally by doctrine equations or tests — for example entire/simple, adjointable, representable, deterministic-causal, or other appropriate laws — rather than by a supplied function list.

### W4 — witness reconstruction

Multiplicity, provenance, cocycles, and higher comparison data survive exactly when admitted future interaction can distinguish them. Reduction is coherent descent, not support collapse.

### W5 — exact composition and Hom reconstruction

The derived typed world has explicit composition and exact cross-object/cross-process Hom reconstruction. A nerve that sees only ambient underlying data is insufficient.

### W6 — doctrine refinement

Changing the experiment doctrine induces the correctly variant comparison between reconstructed worlds. The derived ontology is allowed to change when the available experiments change.

### W7 — geometry from failure

Descent, Beck--Chevalley, holonomy, factorization, or higher-coherence defects should arise as intrinsic obstructions of the reconstructed interaction system rather than being inserted as unrelated diagnostics.

### W8 — blind calibration

At least two mathematically distant worlds must be reconstructed by one rule frozen in advance, without their expected object categories being supplied or named as inputs.

## 3. Expected unicity

Literal categorical uniqueness is probably not the right endpoint. The honest theorem may recover structure only up to

```text
Cauchy completion,
Morita equivalence,
doctrine equivalence,
or a canonical moduli/groupoid of reconstructions.
```

A successful WEIR theorem must identify this ambiguity explicitly.

## 4. Known positive evidence

The category calibration proves an exact special case in which object types, Hom fibres, identities, and composition are downstream of untyped execution plus one-bit continuation success.

Finite response tables prove a second, weaker but doctrine-flexible fact: exact deterministic boundaries are forced by contextual equality, while witness complexity is doctrine-dependent.

Classical allegories, ludics, Interaction Graphs, syntactic algebra, Karoubi completion, and Isbell nuclei show that several individual pieces of the desired architecture are independently natural.

## 5. Known no-go signals

Any general theorem must survive at least these failures:

- a bare distinguishability relation does not determine execution or stable objecthood;
- all idempotents are not a doctrine-free ordinary-object locus;
- scalar support cannot recover witness multiplicity/provenance;
- exact object reconstruction does not imply ULF/factorization lifting;
- dense presentations are not unique merely because their nerves are fully faithful;
- historical primitive signatures need not be recoverable from the full extensional term clone.

These are constraints on the theorem, not annoyances to be hidden.

## 6. Falsification criterion

WEIR should be weakened or abandoned if no natural doctrine package can simultaneously:

1. avoid smuggling in the target object/map structure;
2. reconstruct witnesses and cross-Hom data exactly;
3. behave functorially under the declared recodings;
4. recover distant calibration domains with one frozen rule;
5. state its Cauchy/Morita/doctrine ambiguity honestly.

A collection of domain-specific restatements does not count as WEIR.
