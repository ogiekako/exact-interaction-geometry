# WEIR — Witness-Enriched Interaction Reconstruction

**Status: CONJECTURAL FOUNDATIONAL PROGRAMME.**

WEIR is the current sharp form of the general EIG question. It is intentionally stated as acceptance criteria rather than as a theorem already obtained.

The boundary-first formulation is:

> Given a declared operational interaction doctrine, derive the maximal structure identifiable from all admitted responses, the residual fibre/moduli left observationally invisible, and the additional resource required to reconstruct more.

Literal reconstruction is therefore one successful endpoint, but not the only one. An exact no-go or residual-moduli theorem also counts when the doctrine does not identify a unique target.

## 1. Candidate laboratory

A doctrine `D` should determine a natural class of exact interaction laboratories `L_D` whose primitive data may include:

1. untyped or locally typed interaction fragments and admissible execution/pasting;
2. closed continuation experiments and exact response values;
3. witness-valued response/provenance when an internal boundary can be reopened;
4. recodings under which admitted experiments are required to be invariant.

The expected objects, maps, generators, Hom sets, path categories, span apexes, sectors, metrics, or other target structures must **not** simply be supplied as the answer.

The hoped-for reconstruction has the schematic form

```text
L_D
  -> Syn_D(L)
  -> identifiable core + residual fibre
  -> Intf_D(L) / Map_D(L) / witness-descent structure
```

when those later structures are actually identifiable.

## 2. Acceptance gates

### W1 — declared operational doctrine and no-smuggling boundary

The primitive execution/pasting law, continuation class, response values, allowed recodings, and reopenable witness channels are stated explicitly.

A theorem must not pretend to reconstruct structure that was already supplied as a hidden target label, classifier, factorization, metric, or semantic oracle.

### W2 — exact contextual algebra

There is a coarsest composition-stable reduction preserving every admitted continuation response.

### W3 — maximal identifiable core and residual fibre

The theorem identifies not only what is reconstructible, but also the exact ambiguity left by the doctrine.

The output may be a quotient, stabilizer, fixed-point object, moduli space/groupoid, coarse structure, cohomology class, or another doctrine-relative invariant. If several target realizations remain response-equivalent, that fibre is part of the theorem rather than an implementation detail.

Where possible, identify an additional operational resource whose inclusion strictly reduces or closes the fibre.

### W4 — derived objecthood

Interfaces/objects arise from interaction-internal stable/sharp/retract data selected by the doctrine, not from a supplied object list.

### W5 — derived maps

Map-like interactions are characterized internally by doctrine equations or tests — for example entire/simple, adjointable, representable, deterministic-causal, or other appropriate laws — rather than by a supplied function list.

### W6 — witness reconstruction

Multiplicity, provenance, cocycles, phases, and higher comparison data survive exactly when admitted reopened interaction can distinguish them. Reduction is coherent witness retention/descent, not support collapse.

### W7 — exact composition, Hom reconstruction, and descent

The derived typed world has explicit composition and exact cross-object/cross-process Hom reconstruction when the doctrine supports it. Local witness data glue globally exactly when the corresponding descent conditions hold; failure to glue should produce intrinsic obstruction data rather than an ad hoc diagnostic.

### W8 — doctrine refinement and variance

Changing the experiment doctrine induces the correctly variant comparison between reconstructed worlds and their residual fibres. The derived ontology is allowed to change when the available experiments change.

More observational power may improve identifiability without canonically selecting one representative; those are distinct questions and should not be conflated.

### W9 — blind cross-domain calibration by one frozen meta-rule

At least two mathematically distant worlds must be analyzed by one rule or **meta-rule/schema** frozen in advance, parameterized only by the declared doctrine and not by the target answer.

The requirement is anti-overfitting. It does **not** require every doctrine to use one literal extractor with identical syntax. A mismatch is evidence against the schema, not something to repair by silently inserting target-specific structure after seeing the answer.

## 3. Expected unicity

Literal categorical uniqueness is probably not the right endpoint. The honest theorem may recover structure only up to

```text
isomorphism,
equivalence,
Cauchy completion,
Morita equivalence,
doctrine equivalence,
or a canonical moduli/groupoid of reconstructions.
```

The correct equivalence is itself part of the result.

## 4. Known positive evidence

The category calibration proves an exact special case in which object types, Hom fibres, identities, and composition are downstream of a supplied totalized execution law plus one-bit continuation success. This is not reconstruction of the execution grammar from bare scalar data.

Finite response tables prove a second, weaker but doctrine-flexible fact: exact deterministic residual boundaries are forced by contextual equality, while witness complexity is doctrine-dependent.

The Boolean Tucker example shows that independently minimal local interfaces need not glue through one common exact junction witness; the obstruction is a genuine boundary datum.

The two-state max-plus theorem shows that isolating the exact residual state seen by future interaction can lead to conventional mathematics outside the foundational layer.

Classical allegories, ludics, Interaction Graphs, syntactic algebra, Karoubi completion, Isbell nuclei, behavioral systems, and descent theory show that many individual pieces of the desired architecture are independently natural and largely prior art.

## 5. Known no-go signals

Any general theorem must survive at least these failures:

- a bare distinguishability relation does not determine execution or stable objecthood;
- all idempotents are not a doctrine-free ordinary-object locus;
- scalar support cannot recover witness multiplicity/provenance;
- exact object reconstruction does not imply ULF/factorization lifting;
- dense presentations are not unique merely because their nerves are fully faithful;
- historical primitive signatures need not be recoverable from the full extensional term clone;
- a target may fail to be canonically selected even when a substantial invariant is identifiable;
- a physically preferred factorization or coarse interaction structure need not by itself be an ontic microscopic or metric geometry.

These are constraints on the theorem, not annoyances to hide.

## 6. Falsification criterion

WEIR should be weakened or abandoned if no natural doctrine package can simultaneously:

1. state its supplied execution/response structure without target leakage;
2. derive a nontrivial maximal identifiable core;
3. characterize the residual fibre/moduli honestly;
4. reconstruct witnesses and cross-Hom data when reopened interaction distinguishes them;
5. behave functorially under declared recodings and doctrine refinement;
6. recover distant calibration domains with one frozen meta-rule/schema;
7. produce a real payoff: a reconstruction, a no-go theorem, a reduced hypothesis set, a transferred obstruction, or a new prospective prediction.

A collection of domain-specific restatements does not count as WEIR.
