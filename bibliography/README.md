# Literature map and novelty boundary

This is a targeted map of the closest established mathematics. It is not an exhaustive systematic review. Search absence is not novelty evidence.

## Direct source-pair augmentation question

- M. Parnas and A. Shraibman, **The augmentation property of binary matrices for the binary and Boolean rank**, *Linear Algebra and its Applications* 556 (2018), 70--99. DOI `10.1016/j.laa.2018.07.001`.
- M. Parnas, 2026 survey on Boolean and binary rank, arXiv:2601.13900.

The Section-6 source-pair question is the target of the explicit counterexamples in `papers/source-pair-augmentation/`.

## Graph products and storage normal forms

- Dandan--Gould, **On graph products of monoids**, arXiv:2102.06409 — Foata/reduction theory for arbitrary graph products.
- Yang--Gould, *Journal of Algebra* 620 (2023), 113--156 — graph-product normal forms / one-sided invertibility input used by the GPSH separator work.
- Buckheister--Zetzsche / Zetzsche graph-monoid storage results — recursive tame-storage decomposition and storage-language structure used in Phase III.

**Novelty boundary:** equality/Foata normal form is prior art. The project-specific bridge is right-cone legality, exact shore-local partial dynamics, noncopying separator reconstruction/minimality, recurrent composition, and observer/action transport.

## Open systems and categorical composition

- Baez--Master, **Open Petri Nets**, arXiv:1808.05415.
- Master, **Composing Behaviors of Networks**, arXiv:2105.12905.
- Di Lavore--Gianola--Román--Sabadini--Sobociński, **Span(Graph): a Canonical Feedback Algebra of Open Transition Systems**, arXiv:2010.10069.
- Baez--Courser--Vasilakopoulou, **Structured versus Decorated Cospans**, arXiv:2101.09363.

**Novelty boundary:** cospans, feedback categories, profunctor black-boxing, and Grothendieck constructions are established. The programme's load-bearing issue is exact recurrent codescent with partial storage legality and smaller noncopying interfaces.

## Decomposition and width

- Bumpus--Kocsis--Master, **Structured Decompositions: Structural and Algorithmic Compositionality**, arXiv:2207.06091.
- Di Lavore--Sobociński, **Monoidal Width**, arXiv:2212.13229.
- Althaus--Bumpus--Fairbanks--Rosiak, **Compositional Algorithms on Compositional Data: Deciding Sheaves on Presheaves**, arXiv:2302.05575.

**Novelty boundary:** structured decomposition and categorical width are prior art. The project-specific theorem must derive the admissible process interfaces, exact owner/action ports, and recurrent descent.

## Tangles and separation systems

- Diestel--Erde--Weißauer, **Structural submodularity and tangles in abstract separation systems**, arXiv:1805.01439.

**Novelty boundary:** abstract tangle duality is established once the required structural submodularity/corner closure is proved. The project-specific work is to show that actual process-interface separations satisfy the needed hypotheses, or identify the exact defect when they do not.

## Syntax/dynamics and minimization

- Bonchi--Piedeleu--Sobociński--Zanasi, **Bialgebraic Semantics for String Diagrams**, arXiv:1906.01519.
- Colcombet--Petrișan, **Automata Minimization: a Functorial Approach**, LMCS 16(1:32), 2020.

These calibrate recurrent decoration transport and canonical behavioural quotients; they do not replace the explicit structural interface theorem.

## Profunctors and arities

- Bénabou/Street generalized Grothendieck and lax-functor/profunctor correspondence — prior art.
- Conduché/Giraud exponentiable functors — prior art for pseudo exact-middle factorization.
- Berger--Melliès--Weber, **Monads with arities and their associated theories**, JPAA 216 (2012), arXiv:1101.3064.
- Bourke--Garner, **Monads and theories**, *Advances in Mathematics* 351 (2019), arXiv:1805.04346.

**Novelty boundary:** density, presentable arities, and nervous monads are not programme novelty. The programme-specific target is identification of process-generated reduced interfaces as the relevant arities.

## Curation rule for novelty claims

A future paper should phrase novelty around the **bridge** joining several mature theories while simultaneously respecting:

1. partial storage legality;
2. recurrent boundary recrossing;
3. observer-relative action/cocycle/arithmetic decoration;
4. noncopying separator information;
5. same-query active hardness / exact opaque residuals;
6. process-generated arities.

No single ambient categorical ingredient above should be advertised as new.
