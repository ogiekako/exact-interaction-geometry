# Contemporary neighbours: input/output audit

This note compares EIG with unusually close contemporary programmes by asking a strict question:

> **What mathematical structure is supplied as input, and what structure is actually derived?**

The purpose is not to rank programmes or to manufacture novelty. It is to prevent broad slogans from hiding genuine overlap.

## Comparison rule

Current WEIR asks, in its strongest form, whether one frozen doctrine can start from an interaction laboratory and derive:

1. a coarsest exact all-context reduction;
2. doctrine-relative interfaces / objecthood;
3. internally selected map-like interactions;
4. witness multiplicity / provenance when later interaction can distinguish it;
5. exact cross-object Hom data and composition;
6. functorial doctrine refinement;
7. local-to-global descent and intrinsic obstruction geometry;
8. blind recovery in distant domains without supplying the expected target categories.

A neighbour is therefore not declared equivalent merely because it uses words such as interaction, emergence, quotient, objecthood, lens, descent, or holonomy.

## Six Birds: Foundations of Emergence Calculus

**Reference:** Ioannis Tsiokos, *Six Birds: Foundations of Emergence Calculus*, arXiv:2602.00134 (submitted 2026-01-28).

The foundational package consists, schematically, of a micro-description/process system together with observational **lenses/refinements**, completion/packaging, and audits. Stable objects arise as fixed points of the completion operator. A bounded-interface theorem organizes the six primitives, and route mismatch / holonomy measures noncommutation between different coarse-graining or completion routes.

This is serious prior art for:

- process/emergence-first foundations;
- bounded observational interfaces;
- lens-relative description;
- idempotent completion and fixed-point object formation;
- monotone audits and data processing;
- route-mismatch / holonomy as a structural diagnostic.

The later finite-state substrate strengthens the comparison. It explicitly prohibits externally declared quotient objects and instead derives packaging equivalence from an internal packaging endomap inside a finite autonomous stochastic machine. Thus it is inaccurate to dismiss Six Birds as simply “assuming the objects”.

The remaining distinction is narrower. Even the no-smuggling substrate retains an admissible deterministic lens family as part of the substrate. EIG's open target is to ask when the relevant exact interface/object/map structure itself is forced by admitted continuations and responses, and to retain witness/Hom data that a quotient or fixed-point packaging may erase.

**Audit verdict:** large overlap; close contemporary neighbour; not currently identified as equivalent to WEIR.

## Youvan: stable quotients and distributed objecthood

**References:** Douglas C. Youvan, July 2026 series, especially *Object Formation and Conceptual Resolution* and *Local Observation and Global Objecthood: Sheaf Descent, Gluing Obstructions, and the Algebra of Distributed Concept Formation*.

The local-to-global theory starts with a state space `X`, a cover `{U_i}`, and supplied local equivalence policies `E_i`. Each quotient `U_i/E_i` is a local object space. The canonical globalization is

```text
E* = EqCl(union_i E_i),
```

and faithful descent holds exactly when `E*|U_i = E_i` for every domain. Returning zig-zags are obstruction witnesses. Compatible local transformations can assemble and descend to the global quotient, and the lattices `Eq(U)` form a contravariant presheaf of resolutions.

This is direct prior art for:

- policy-relative quotient objecthood;
- local-to-global object formation;
- exact faithful-descent criteria;
- gluing defects and returning-zig-zag witnesses;
- transformation-stable global quotients;
- presheaf organization of local resolutions.

The paper is also unusually useful because it states its limitations explicitly. It says that the local/global equivalence relations are supplied policies, not yet selected or learned by the theory; that quotienting erases provenance and multiplicity of identity paths; and that groupoids, enriched categories, stacks, higher coherence, and provenance-sensitive witnesses are later extensions. Its strict network morphisms also use supplied functions and index maps.

This makes the difference from WEIR precise. Youvan supplies local identity policies and then solves their exact globalization/descent problem. WEIR asks, more strongly, when the identity/interface policy itself and the witness-enriched typed world can be reconstructed from interaction data.

**Audit verdict:** very close on quotient objecthood and descent; explicitly not yet witness-enriched or policy-generating in the EIG sense.

## Youvan: Algebraic Abiogenesis / Mathematical It from Bit

**Reference:** Douglas C. Youvan, *Mathematical It from Bit: Algebraic Abiogenesis, Anonymous Finite Operations, and the Automated Birth of Abstract Algebra*; public repository `DougYouvan/algebraic-abiogenesis`.

This line is particularly close to EIG's operation-first algebraic sector. It starts with an anonymous finite signature and finite operation tables, evaluates terms extensionally, defines equality of terms by equality of term functions across the model ecology, and quotients the free term algebra by the resulting fully invariant congruence. Countermodels refine the quotient monotonically and basis compression produces an equational theory.

This is direct prior art for:

```text
anonymous operations
    -> exact extensional observation
    -> stable congruence
    -> quotient algebraic objects / laws.
```

The supplied structure is still substantial: a signature, variable set, term syntax, and family of finite algebras. The output is an equational/free-algebra world. WEIR's target is broader and differently typed: derive object/interface loci, internal maps, exact cross-Hom data, witness provenance, doctrine change, and descent from a more general interaction laboratory.

**Audit verdict:** genuine operation-first reconstruction precedent; does not presently subsume the WEIR package.

## Wang: Fiber Fingerprints of Hidden Learning-State Dynamics

**Reference:** Qinyou Wang, *Fiber Fingerprints of Hidden Learning-State Dynamics*, arXiv:2608.15976 (2026-08-17).

A declared category of execution contexts, a state functor, protocols, a present readout, and future probes define predictive equivalence by agreement under all future probes. The quotient is minimal in a Nerode sense, descends along execution structure, and can be accompanied by fibres / conditional realizations.

This is direct contemporary prior art for:

- all-future-probe contextual equality;
- a canonical minimal predictive quotient;
- quotient descent;
- retention of useful fibre information beyond the quotient class.

The difference is again at the input boundary: the execution-context category, state functor, protocols, and probe doctrine are already supplied. WEIR asks when the object/interface/map world is itself downstream of raw interaction and admitted response.

**Audit verdict:** one of the closest exact-contextual-reduction neighbours; not currently a full object/Hom/witness/descent reconstruction theorem.

## Why the distinction is not a slogan

The remaining EIG target should be phrased negatively as much as positively.

EIG is **not** claiming novelty for any of the following in isolation:

- interaction or process as primitive;
- objects as quotients or fixed points;
- observational or behavioral equivalence;
- all-future-context minimization;
- idempotent completion;
- local-to-global descent;
- gluing/holonomy defects;
- fibres/provenance as useful retained data;
- anonymous operations giving rise to quotient algebraic theories.

The narrow open claim is about **simultaneity and derivation boundary**: can a natural doctrine start with one interaction laboratory and derive the exact contextual algebra, object/interface locus, internal maps, witness-enriched Hom/composition, doctrine refinement, and descent geometry without separately supplying the answer at each stage?

That is why WEIR is written as acceptance gates rather than as a slogan.

## Current historical posture

This audit is deliberately conservative:

- no claim is made that this neighbour list is historically exhaustive;
- no claim is made that the WEIR package is proven unique or unprecedented;
- close 2026 work is cited even when peer-review status is unsettled, because priority and conceptual overlap do not depend on journal publication;
- a future equivalence theorem identifying EIG with an existing framework would be a legitimate research result, not something to conceal.

At the present snapshot, the audit has found several frameworks covering large pieces of WEIR but has **not identified a direct predecessor that derives the entire gate package from one frozen interaction laboratory**.
