# Contemporary neighbours: input/output comparison

**Last reviewed: 2026-09-01**

This note compares EIG with unusually close contemporary programmes by asking a strict question:

> **What mathematical structure is supplied as input, and what structure is actually derived?**

The purpose is not to rank programmes or manufacture novelty. Statements about what a cited source says are distinguished from this repository's interpretation of overlap with EIG.

## Comparison rule

Current WEIR asks, in its strongest form, whether one frozen doctrine can start from an interaction laboratory and derive:

1. a coarsest exact all-context reduction;
2. doctrine-relative interfaces / objecthood;
3. internally selected map-like interactions;
4. witness multiplicity / provenance when later interaction can distinguish it;
5. exact cross-object Hom data and composition;
6. functorial doctrine refinement;
7. local-to-global descent and intrinsic obstruction geometry;
8. blind recovery in distant domains without supplying the expected target categories.

A neighbour is therefore not declared equivalent merely because it uses words such as interaction, emergence, quotient, objecthood, lens, descent, witness, policy, or holonomy.

## Six Birds: Foundations of Emergence Calculus

**Reference:** Ioannis Tsiokos, *Six Birds: Foundations of Emergence Calculus*, arXiv:2602.00134 (submitted 2026-01-28).

The cited work studies a micro-description/process system together with observational lenses/refinements, completion/packaging, and audits. Stable objects arise as fixed points of completion, and route mismatch / holonomy is used to study noncommutation between routes.

We regard this as relevant prior art for process/emergence-first foundations, observational interfaces, idempotent packaging, fixed-point object formation, and route-mismatch diagnostics. This is an EIG comparison judgment, not a claim made by the source about EIG.

The later finite-state substrate describes a no-smuggling discipline in which quotienting is internally induced rather than externally declared. It would therefore be inaccurate to dismiss Six Birds simply as “assuming the objects”.

The remaining distinction is narrower. In the formulation reviewed here, an admissible deterministic lens family remains part of the substrate. EIG's open target asks when exact interface/object/map structure itself is forced by admitted continuations and responses, with witness/Hom data retained when a quotient would erase them.

**Repository interpretation:** substantial conceptual overlap; not identified here as theorem-level equivalent to WEIR.

## Youvan 2026: stable quotients, admissibility, certified witnesses, and policy genesis

Douglas C. Youvan's July 2026 sequence is recorded here as a possible close conceptual neighbour. These are recent author-uploaded works; this repository does not treat their existence as independent validation of any EIG claim. Where detailed text was not directly available for verification, the comparison should be regarded as provisional rather than reconstructed from titles, snippets, or memory.

Relevant items include:

- *Object Formation and Conceptual Resolution: Stable Quotients, Complete Refinement Lattices, and Functorial Observation*, DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.25921.72800`;
- *Local Observation and Global Objecthood: Sheaf Descent, Gluing Obstructions, and the Algebra of Distributed Concept Formation*, DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.12748.99204`;
- *Admissibility Algebra: A Policy-Relative Foundation for Identity, Invariance, and Certified Transformation*, DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.28510.40006`;
- *Admissibility Fibrations: Policy-Indexed Identity, Certified Quotients, and Lyapunov-Stable Emergence*, DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.11130.25283`;
- *Categorical Neural Abiogenesis: Policy-Indexed Quotients, Functorial Compression, and Certified Memory in No-Corpus Artificial Intelligence*, DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.36315.73765`;
- *Equivalence Genesis and Recursive Ontogenesis: Obstruction-Driven Identity Policies Beyond Homotopy Equivalence and Univalence*, DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.17359.44968`.

At the level of the material reviewed for this repository, these works use related language concerning policy-relative quotienting, object formation, admissibility, certificates/witnesses, refinement, gluing, and obstruction-driven policy formation. We therefore record those themes as possible areas of overlap and avoid claiming novelty for them in isolation.

The exact theorem-level relationship remains unsettled here. In particular, this note does not claim that Youvan's framework is formally equivalent to EIG, that it is a direct predecessor of any specific EIG theorem, or that phrases such as “very large overlap” have been externally established.

The remaining distinction is an EIG-side interpretation of the derivation boundary. In the formulations reviewed here, substantial structure appears supplied or parameterized: presentations and candidate transformations, policy/admissibility machinery, invariant languages, certificate/coherence data, contexts/tasks, and in some constructions signatures or term syntax. We have not identified, in the material reviewed for this snapshot, a theorem that begins from one WEIR-style primitive laboratory of execution/pasting plus admitted continuation response and then, by one frozen rule, simultaneously derives:

- the coarsest exact all-context contextual algebra;
- the intended interface/object locus;
- internally selected map-like interactions;
- exact reopened-boundary witness provenance;
- cross-object Hom data and composition;
- doctrine change;
- intrinsic descent geometry;
- and blind recovery in mathematically distant calibration domains.

This is a bounded literature comparison, not a universal priority judgment.

**Repository interpretation:** possible close conceptual neighbour across several WEIR gates; exact equivalence and novelty boundary remain provisional.

## Youvan: Algebraic Abiogenesis / Mathematical It from Bit

**Reference:** Douglas C. Youvan, *Mathematical It from Bit: Algebraic Abiogenesis, Anonymous Finite Operations, and the Automated Birth of Abstract Algebra*; public repository `DougYouvan/algebraic-abiogenesis`.

The accessible description starts from an anonymous finite signature and finite operation tables, evaluates terms extensionally, and organizes terms by extensional equality across finite models. We regard this as relevant to EIG's operation-first algebraic sector.

The supplied structure is still substantial: a signature, variable set, term syntax, and family of finite algebras. The output is an equational/free-algebra world. WEIR's target is broader and differently typed: derive object/interface loci, internal maps, exact cross-Hom data, witness provenance, doctrine change, and descent from a more general interaction laboratory.

**Repository interpretation:** an operation-first reconstruction precedent at the conceptual level; no claim of formal subsumption or equivalence is made here.

## Wang: Fiber Fingerprints of Hidden Learning-State Dynamics

**Reference:** Qinyou Wang, *Fiber Fingerprints of Hidden Learning-State Dynamics*, arXiv:2608.15976 (2026-08-17).

A declared category of execution contexts, a state functor, protocols, a present readout, and future probes define predictive equivalence by agreement under all future probes. The quotient is minimal in a Nerode sense, descends along execution structure, and can be accompanied by fibres / conditional realizations.

We regard this as relevant prior art for all-future-probe contextual equality, minimal predictive quotients, quotient descent, and retained fibre information. The difference is again at the input boundary: the execution-context category, state functor, protocols, and probe doctrine are already supplied, while WEIR asks when an object/interface/map world can itself be downstream of raw interaction and admitted response.

**Repository interpretation:** one of the closest exact-contextual-reduction neighbours identified in this review; not claimed here to be a full object/Hom/witness/descent reconstruction theorem.

## Why the distinction is not a slogan

EIG is **not** claiming novelty for any of the following in isolation:

- interaction or process as primitive;
- objects as quotients or fixed points;
- observational or behavioral equivalence;
- all-future-context minimization;
- idempotent completion;
- policy-indexed identity or objecthood;
- proof-relevant certificates, witness multiplicity, or higher coherence as such;
- functorial refinement/change of identity policy;
- obstruction-driven refinement or generation of identity policies;
- local-to-global descent;
- gluing/holonomy defects;
- fibres/provenance as useful retained data;
- anonymous operations giving rise to quotient algebraic theories.

The narrow open question is about **simultaneity and derivation boundary**: can a natural doctrine start with one interaction laboratory and derive the exact contextual algebra, object/interface locus, internal maps, witness-enriched Hom/composition, doctrine refinement, and descent geometry without separately supplying the answer at each stage, and can one frozen rule survive blind calibration in structurally distant domains?

That is why WEIR is written as acceptance gates rather than as a slogan.

## Current historical posture

This comparison is deliberately conservative:

- no claim is made that this neighbour list is historically exhaustive;
- no claim is made that the WEIR package is proven unique or unprecedented;
- recent author-uploaded or non-peer-reviewed work may still be relevant to priority, but its authority and technical overlap are not assumed from publication status alone;
- a future equivalence theorem identifying EIG with an existing framework would be a legitimate research result, not something to conceal.

At the present snapshot, the literature reviewed here contains several frameworks covering important pieces of WEIR. We have not identified a source proving the exact entire gate package from one frozen interaction laboratory. That statement is bounded to this review and is not a certificate of historical firstness.
